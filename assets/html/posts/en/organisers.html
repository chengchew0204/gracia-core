A meeting ground for communities

CAMP is a supportive and safe(r) setting for events, classes, immersions, dance, theatre, film, participatory art, gatherings, episodic communities, and small 'festivals' of up to 200.

FOR ORGANISERS:


Values

Our curation is based on these values:

  • Corporeal and incorporeal productions remind you of the full spectrum and multiplicity of human experience; exploring layers and dimensions of the self and the different kinds of interactions you can have. Favoured happenings are unsacred original (or reconsidered) systems of knowledge: instead of re-teaching a familiar modality, maybe make a metabolisation that is new, yours, ... poiesis.
  • Affinity: persons of any identity or expression are welcome in CAMP, and Zipolite.
  • Transparency and consent: this is a setting for safe(r) trainings, happenings, and workshops.
  • Woo: access spirit without misappropriating or sacralizing your work in Original Peoples' terminology.
  • Trauma sensitivity and resilience: center practices such as setting clear boundaries, fostering open communication, and prioritising well-being.

Facts


  • Up to 52 shared or private bedspaces (34 lower, 18 upper) in the Retreat.
  • 8 gathering / teaching spaces (almost 700 m² / 7,000 ft²): forum and fogata gardens (90 m² ea); studio (120 m²); shala (90 m²); sound-tight black box theatre (60 m²) with air conditioning, stage lighting, sound system, and projector; salon (80 m²); lounge (60 m²); sala de meditación (40 m²).
  • SENEM vegetarian meals are included in pricing.
  • Communal kitchens, refrigerators, implements, ware, and some staple pantry ingredients.
  • Steps to eating places, shopping, craft vendors, adjacent beach Bahía Camarón, and the country's only clothing-optional beaches: Playa Oeste, Playa Centro, Playa del Amor.
  • Warmed sand bottom mineral pool for reflection, conversation, aquatherapy, Flothetta, hydrotherapy.
  • 10-person sauna.
  • Private air-conditioned bodywork / pleasure space, la Sala.
  • Restaurant.
  • Telmex fiber and Starlink satellite WiFi.

Organiser Advice

It is important that Organiser/s read and agree to these conditions (including hover details):

  • ASSISTANTS:
    Have sufficient help to attend the door, and to restore, clean, and leave CAMP as you found it (the organiser or assistant/s must remain until all participants depart).
  • AMPLIFICATION UNTIL SUNSET:
    By agreement with our neighbours and municipality, amplified sound is limited to afternoons 3:00pm–sunset. Acoustic and tempered sound (no drum/s) is permitted after sunset until 9:00pm (use the sound-tight theatre after 9:00 pm). We notify our neighbours days in advance when an activity will involve amplification. Arrive early for system setup and brief soundcheck.
  • ACTIVITIES END 22:00:
    Facilitation, verbal group engagement, applause, etc. ends at 22:00 (10:00pm) when restoration of furniture, hammocks, and cleanup can begin and end before 23:00 (11:00pm).
  • PUBLICITY:
    Create digital art or a reel for our calendar and social media; <2,200-character caption including: date, start / end times, @handle/s, price; as CAMP enjoys a follower base, it may be enrolling to include a discreet logo (download logos here), this is not mandatory. QR code/s in online publicity is not useful. CAMP is just named CAMP or if you prefer, say CAMP, Zipolite (the comma is important). Submit proposed details and digital art for approval not less than 10 days before a desired date and notify us of any changed details before updating publicity. Collaborator-tag @campmx in your social media posts and stories.
  • SPANISH-ALSO:
    CAMP is gratefully committed to embracing local representation at happenings here. When an offering will only be facilitated in English, say so (many latinxs speak English), and supply a Spanish version of digital art and caption. Sometimes, cleverly merged Spanish+English art with caption translations can work. The point is to transmit a welcoming invitation to people of the region. It's ok to preserve an event's English title in otherwise translated material. Events conducted in Spanish do not need English-language publicity.
  • PHOTOGRAPHY:
    We may offer useful advice if you intend to include a professional or purposive photographer within your event, workshop, or retreat.
  • PRIVATE EVENTS, PRIVATE RETREATS:
    An unpublished private retreat or event (which does not identify CAMP in advertisements) may bypass some of our selection criteria. If you choose to advertise it yourself, you may not identify CAMP in your advertisements unless this has been approved by us in advance.
  • SPONSORSHIPS (optional):
    Some retreats and happenings may feature, or qualify entirely for, CAMP-supported sponsorships to subsidise underrepresented regional, ability, socioeconomic, LGBTQIA+, and global majority participation.

Essentials to share with participants

For important practical and safety information about behaviour, parking, drinking water, kitchen, sauna, alcohol, nudity, families, etc. (and to reduce Organiser's risk), see the Things to know and how to be in CAMP guide. Accordingly, select and display pertinent guidelines for your participants to read before or upon arrival.
